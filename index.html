What We Do

We believe that self-understanding is the foundation of a meaningful life. Know Thyself builds tools that empower people to explore their habits, track what matters, and uncover the patterns that shape who they are.

Our first product is Cause — a personal tracking app that puts the power of self-discovery in your hands.
